Examples

Intro

intro plays one state first, the first time the icon is in view; then the trigger takes over. Reload the page to see them again. 

intro trigger="hover"

The first in-* state, then a hover.

intro="hover-jump" trigger="hover"

A state named by the attribute.

intro="delay=800" trigger="hover"

A pause before the intro.

intro trigger="loop" state="loop-spin"

The entrance, then the loop.

An icon is ready before its intro plays, and a method call such as play() cuts the intro short. See also intro after an animation and intro out of view.
