Examples

Sequence

A script of steps, separated by commas: play, show, wait and stop. It repeats unless it ends with stop. 

trigger="sequence(
    play in-reveal,
    wait 500,
    play hover-jump,
    wait 1000
)"

Two states, one after the other, with pauses.

trigger="sequence(
    play morph-select there,
    wait 1000,
    play morph-select back,
    wait 1000
)"

The two halves of a morph: to the second look, and back.

trigger="sequence(
    play morph-unlocked,
    wait 1000,
    play morph-unlocked reverse,
    wait 1000
)"

A morph without a ratio: forwards, then backwards.

trigger="sequence(
    play in-reveal 0-60,
    wait 800,
    play in-reveal 61-end,
    wait 800
)"

Part of a state: frames counted from its start, both ends included.

trigger="sequence(
    play in-reveal,
    stop
)"

Plays once and stops.

trigger="sequence(
    show morph-select there
)"

A still: the morph's second look. A script with no play or wait runs once. 

trigger="sequence(
    show in-reveal end
)"

A still: the last frame of a state.
