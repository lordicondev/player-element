Examples

Reduced motion

What each kind of trigger does when the viewer asks for less motion. Your system asks: no. Switch the setting; the icons follow without a reload. 

trigger="loop"

Stays still.

trigger="loop" motion="always"

Loops anyway.

trigger="morph" state="morph-unlocked"

Jumps to the other look.

trigger="in" state="in-reveal"

Shows the last frame.

defineElement({ motion: 'always' }) does the same for every icon on the page; motion="auto" on one icon brings the default back for it.
