Examples

Target

target names an ancestor the trigger listens to. Here each row is the target: hover anywhere on it. 

trigger="hover" target=".row"

Plays when the pointer enters the row.

trigger="loop-on-hover" target=".row"

Loops while the pointer is on the row.

trigger="morph" state="morph-unlocked" target=".row"

Unlocks while the pointer is on the row.
