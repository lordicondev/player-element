Examples

Loading on interaction

loading="interaction" waits for the first pointer, click or keyboard focus on the icon, then loads it and plays. An image inside stands in until then. 

loading="interaction" trigger="hover"
  <img src="lock.svg" />

Hover: the icon loads, then plays.

loading="interaction" trigger="morph" state="morph-detach"
  <img src="puzzle.svg" />

Works with any trigger.

loading="interaction" trigger="hover"
await icon.load(); icon.play()

load() loads the icon now, whatever it waits for, and resolves once it is ready.
