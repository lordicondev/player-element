Examples

Follow

follow keeps the icon in step with an attribute on the target: a pressed button, a selected item, a count, a stage of work.

A toggle button

trigger="follow" target="button" follows aria-pressed: a morph goes to its second look when the button is pressed. Click the buttons.

A selected item

trigger="follow(aria-selected)" target="div". Click a row.

A stage of work

trigger="follow(data-stage, busy=loop-cycle, done=morph-check)": a state per value. The loop finishes its round before the next state plays; the morph goes to its check and back when the stage returns to idle.
