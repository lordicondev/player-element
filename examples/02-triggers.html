Triggers

The built-in triggers, one per row. follow and sequence have pages of their own. 

trigger="hover"

Plays once when the pointer enters, or keyboard focus lands on the icon.

trigger="click"

Plays on every click.

trigger="in" state="in-reveal"

Plays once, when half of the icon is in view.

trigger="in(1000)" state="in-reveal"

The same, a second later.

trigger="loop"

Plays over and over.

trigger="loop(1000)"

With a pause of a second between rounds.

trigger="loop-on-hover"

Loops while the pointer is over the icon; the round in progress finishes.

trigger="morph" state="morph-select"

Goes to a second look while the pointer is over the icon, and back when it leaves. This morph has a ratio in its marker: the way there and the way back are two halves. 

trigger="morph" state="morph-unlocked"

A morph without a ratio: forwards on the way there, backwards on the way back. 

trigger="boomerang" state="morph-select"

There and straight back when the pointer enters.
